Entropia Archive

The Entropia Archive is a community project that preserves data related to Project Entropia by MindArk. Its sole purpose is the historical preservation of this media. Below are the Version Updates (VU) of Project Entropia, and of Entropia Universe up to VU 10: their release notes, which clients survive, and where each client file was found.

Did you play Project Entropia in its early years, or do you collect games and magazine cover discs from that era? An old installer, a promotional CD or a forgotten cover disc could bring a lost version back. If you have any of these, or anything else from that time, please email thecarow@gmail.com or message Ethar on the Entropia Forum.
